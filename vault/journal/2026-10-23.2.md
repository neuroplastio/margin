# 2026-10-23.2 — Resources again after a lost document

Kind: mech
Task: (resources after a lost document), from the maintainer's report that
margin loses its images when plx restarts.
Commit: (this one)

## What changed

- **hotty-go's hottytea and hottytest are at 2ebf3ef** (untagged, the
  pseudo-version v0.5.1-0.20261023074741-2ebf3ef12190). The root module
  stays at v0.5.0. Move all three to v0.5.1 once the maintainer tags it.
- **On `RelayoutMsg{Lost: true}` margin sends its resources again.**
  hottytea says Lost when a placement finds its document gone (ENOENT), and
  the frame's Layout sends the documents again. Before that Layout, in
  `hotUpdate`, margin sends the stylesheet again and every diagram SVG it
  has drawn (`hotDiagram` now keeps its SVG), and drops the cached block
  documents, so making them again reads their images and sends those
  again. Everything goes out in the same Flush, resources first.

## Tests

`make check` and `make test-race`. `TestHottyLostResourcesGoAgain`
loads a document with a picture, a mermaid flowchart and 40 paragraphs,
then calls `Host.Forget()` and scrolls a page down and back up. For the
stylesheet, the picture and the diagram SVG, the first `a=res` after the
loss must come before the first `a=doc` of the surface that refers to it.
With `h.docs.lost()` taken out the picture and diagram checks fail; with
the stylesheet line taken out, the stylesheet check fails. 60 of 60 runs
pass.

The restart itself is still not reproduced: plx replays the whole session
on a handover and on a viewport re-exec, so no host lost anything in the
cases I tried. This makes margin right for a host that does lose them.

## Notes

- **One Forget gave two Lost reports.** A surface that was off screen when
  the loss was reported comes back later, finds its document gone too, and
  counts as a new loss. Sending again is harmless, but after a real
  restart, each scroll that brings such surfaces back would send every
  resource again, images up to 8 MB each. This is going to the hotty agent
  as a suggestion: on a reported loss, hottytea could treat every surface
  as having no document, so later placements send theirs without another
  ENOENT.
- **Two flaky tests are fixed in their own commits.**
  - `TestHottyFitGrowsABlock` read the fit flag from the last placement,
    but hottytea sends `f` only when it changes, so a later placement
    leaves it out (2 failures in 80 runs). It now reads the surface's
    `Fit()` (731e9fa).
  - `TestCtrlEnterDecodesThroughRealReader` raced the reader's EOF against
    its event (19 failures in 200 runs under -race). It now takes the
    event still in the channel (ef5e02a).
- **Glide rows on plx c0871f93** (host events count as input), 40 trials
  each, 0 misses, p50/p90 ms:
