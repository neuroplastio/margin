# 2026-10-23.8 — The attach surface goes when input measures its block

Kind: mech
Task: the plx agent asked me to run its ENOTERM test build
(26.10.23-dev.90ef21f, a local branch) against margin. I ran margin from
main against it headless (`bench/enoterm.sh` in the session scratchpad).
Commit: (this one)

## What the run showed

- margin started with a viewer attached. The viewer was then detached by
  killing hottyterm. The document got a new paragraph, and ctrl+r was
  written into the pane.
- The paragraph's measure was answered ENOTERM within 9 ms.
  `margin-attach` was placed and answered ENOTERM too. No error showed.
- A viewer attached again. For 6 s with no input, no `fit` reached margin
  for `margin-attach`, and the paragraph stayed in cells. plx's replay
  showed every other block as a surface. The missing fit was reported
  to the plx agent, with the trace and the placement's parameters.
- A pointer move asked again (2026-10-23.4): the paragraph was measured,
  and it showed as a surface.

## What changed

That last step left `margin-attach` placed and `attach` set. margin went
on waiting for a fit for a block that already had rows. When an ordinary
measure now gives the attach block rows, the surface is deleted and
`attach` is cleared. `unattach` is shared with the fit path. It sends the
delete only if the surface was placed, so it sends none after a Lost.

## Tests

`make check` and `make test-race` pass. `TestHotMeasureWaitsForATerminal`
now goes on past the fit:
- block c is answered ENOTERM, and the frame places `margin-attach`;
- a key asks c again, and the reply has rows;
- `attach` must then be cleared and `attachN` must be 0.

It fails without the change.
