# 2026-10-02.6 — A block grows when its image arrives late (fit)

Kind: felt (how the page moves when a block grows needs the maintainer's
eyes; that it grows is mechanical and tested).
Task: maintainer direction — "update the spec and the protocol to close
image resize gap".
Commit: (this one)

## What changed

- **Blocks are placed asking for `fit`** when the host's capabilities list
  it (SPEC §5.2 `f=1`, §9): the host then says, with the rows, when a
  block's document needs other rows than it was placed with.
- **A `fit` is a measurement.** Its rows replace the block's rows at the
  column's width, and the page is laid out again on the next relayout tick,
  the top visible block held still as after a measuring wave.

## Why

Each block is measured once on a throwaway surface. An image that decodes
after that, or a web image that loads later, made the block taller than its
rows, and the host clipped it. The protocol had no way to say so. `fit` is
the new event for it, specified in neuroplastio/hotty (SPEC §5.2, §9) and
implemented in hotty-go (`Placement.Fit`, `Event.FitRows`, the test host),
hotty-blitz, the xterm.js addon and the plx relay (plexos HOTTY-FIT-01).

## Tests

- `TestHottyFitGrowsABlock`: the quote is placed with `Fit` and its measured
  2 rows; after the host's `fit` with 5 it is placed again with 5, and the
  heading below it moves down 3. With the rows not taken, the test fails.

## Demo recipe

In hottyterm from its fit release, `margin testdata/demo/demo.md`:

1. With https images allowed, put a web image in the document
   (`![x](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/47/PNG_transparency_demonstration_1.png)`):
   its paragraph is a row or two while the image loads, then grows to show
   it whole.
2. The PNG and the GIF don't move: margin writes their size from the file
   into the document, so the first measure already has it.
