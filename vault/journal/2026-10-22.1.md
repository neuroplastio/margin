# 2026-10-22.1 — The footer's keys inside its row on a host

Kind: felt (on a HOTTY host the footer now shows its whole line, where it
showed the top half of it, or nothing in a plx pane).
Task: maintainer report — margin on `testdata/mermaid.md` in hottyterm and
in a plx pane, "key shortcuts are outside of the viewport, not visible";
"Coordinate with hotty agent to fix margin+kit issue, ship it".
Commit: (this one)

## What changed

- **The footer is a dense surface.** hotty-a2ui's `html.Rendition` gained
  `SetDense` (kit `78a9985`, then `d6d7db1` so a bar never wraps), made by
  the hotty agent at margin's request. margin's panes get a `bar` flag; the
  footer sets it, and its HTML rendition is made dense. The palette, the
  search prompt and the other panes are not bars and stay as they were.
- **Why it was cut (F35):** the kit's surface has 12px of padding and a
  line height of 1.5, so on a one-row (20px) surface the footer's text
  started about 21px down. hottyterm drew the top of it in the window's
  padding below the last row; a plx pane clips the surface to its row
  exactly, so there it was blank. In a 60-column plx pane the caption
  ("0/16 reviewed") also wrapped beside the key hints, making the bar two
  lines tall even dense; the kit's follow-up keeps a dense bar on one line
  and lets the hints, not the caption, give way.
- hotty-a2ui pinned at `d6d7db1`; hotty-go and its submodules follow it to
  v0.4.0 (the same code as `0f4c521`).

## Tests

`make check` and `make test-race`. `TestHottyFooterIsADenseBar`: on a host
the footer is placed one row tall and its surface carries `k-dense`; the
palette, opened over it, does not. It fails with the footer's `bar` off.

Looked at in a private headless sway (hottyterm at 2400×1500, scale 2) on
`testdata/mermaid.md`, before and after, straight in hottyterm and in a
60-column plx pane beside a neighbour: the line now sits in its row in
both; in the pane the hints cut after "Y copy" and "0/16 reviewed" is
whole. `hotty render --rows auto` of the footer's own document gives 1 row
at 40, 60 and 119 columns.

## Notes

- The search prompt has the same fault and is not fixed here: on a host its
  label and field need about 6 rows (4 dense), and margin places it over
  the 2 it uses in cells, so the field is cut. It needs either a one-row
  field with an inline label from the kit or margin giving it more rows; a
  leg of its own.
- The rest of the maintainer's report (measure surfaces drawn outside the
  pane, every measure halting the pane's output) is plx's, reported to the
  plx agent 2026-10-21.
