## What changed

- **The search prompt is a dense surface.** margin places it over the two
  rows it has in cells, but the kit's HTML put a field's label above its
  input, 2.25rem tall with padding: 5 rows, 3 dense. At margin's request
  the hotty agent made a dense one-line field lay out as cells does (kit
  `cf085ff`): the label beside the input, the input a row tall, the Divider
  without its margin. margin sets the search pane's `bar`, as the footer's.
- hotty-a2ui pinned at `cf085ff`. F35 updated: a pane placed over its cells
  rows, fields and all, is a bar.

## Tests

`make check` and `make test-race`. `TestHottySearchIsASurface` now also
checks the prompt is placed two rows tall and its surface carries
`k-dense`.

`hotty render --rows auto --cell 9.5x20` of the search pane's own document:
2 rows at 40, 60 and 119 columns, the rule on the first and "/", the field
and the count on the second; the footer's still 1. Looked at in a private
headless sway (hottyterm at 2400×1500, scale 2) on `testdata/mermaid.md`,
`/` then `push`: straight in hottyterm and in a 60-column plx pane, the
rule, "/", the field holding "push" and "1/2" are whole in both.

## Notes

  row under it is blank, as in cells.
