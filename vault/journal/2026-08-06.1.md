# 2026-08-06.1 — M0: the interaction model, end to end

Kind: felt
Task: M0
Commit: (initial)

## What changed

margin exists. A seeded document renders as a block list with anchored comment
threads, and the whole review loop works from the keyboard:

- Focusing a thread splices a real `nvim` into the document as the composer,
  stripped to a textarea. Blurring it kills the child and keeps the text as a
  draft keyed by anchor and target; refocusing respawns nvim where you left off.
- `c` writes a new comment, `e` edits the focused one in place. Insert mode iff
  the buffer is empty.
- `r` marks reviewed and `f` flags for later; on a heading, the whole section.
- Redraws are damage-driven, and the host round trip is measured rather than
  guessed.

Threads are blocks: collapsed, expanded and composing are three renderings of one
list, and a single render pass produces both the lines and the spans that click
routing, cursor placement and scrolling all read from.

## Notes

This milestone was built interactively with the maintainer at the terminal, which
is why it moved fast and why the loop from here on is deliberately slower. Four
separate defects were found by a human looking at the screen and could not have
been caught by tests: the cursor sitting one row above the text, a block cursor
that never became a bar in insert mode, capitals silently vanishing, and 50–100ms
of felt lag. All four are recorded in `knowledge/findings.md`.

The document is seeded in code. There is no markdown parser yet, no file loading,
and no thread persistence — that is M1 and M2.

Dead end worth recording: the composer's buffer originally carried instructions
and the quoted block below a `git`-style scissors fold. It was cut. The block is
already on screen directly above the pane and the keys are in the footer, so both
were clutter to scroll past in a ten-row window.

## Demo

    make run

Judge:
- Does the prose read comfortably, or does the chrome crowd it?
- `j`/`k` to a thread, then `e` on a comment — does it edit in place?
- Type into a composer, click a paragraph elsewhere, then come back — is the
  draft exactly where you left it?
- `r` on a heading — does the section roll-up read at a glance while scanning?
