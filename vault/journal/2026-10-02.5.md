# 2026-10-02.5 — Mermaid diagrams as SVG on a HOTTY host

Kind: felt (how the diagrams look beside the prose needs the maintainer's
eyes; the renderer and the plumbing are mechanical and tested).
Task: maintainer direction — "I want images, gifs and mermaid diagrams to
render via hotty".
Commit: (this one, and the renderer's before it)

## What changed

- **`internal/mermaidsvg`** (the commit before this one): mermaid source to
  SVG in-process. merman, a Rust port of Mermaid 12, is compiled to WASI and
  embedded gzipped, and runs under wazero. D19 has the choice and its costs.
- **A mermaid fence on a host is a surface.** Its document is an `<img>` of
  the SVG, sent once as a resource and sized from the SVG's root. Until the
  SVG is drawn, and for a diagram merman cannot draw (an unsupported kind),
  the block is the cells it always was. D17 amended.
- `MARGIN_HOTTY_LOG` notes each diagram drawn (bytes, time) or why not.

## Notes

merman was chosen over three other Rust renderers by building each for
WASI and rasterizing the output with resvg (D19's Rejected). The agent that
did that found wazero's amd64 compiler miscompiling one float operation in
every tagged release since v1.9.0, so wazero is pinned to a commit on its
main branch until a release has the fix.

Checked in hottyterm on the headless display with `testdata/demo/`'s three
diagrams: cells for the first 2.7 s (the compile, cold), then all three as
SVG, measured and placed in 20 ms. One defect, in the flowchart: the
`doc --> margin` edge shows its arrowhead and no line. merman, usvg or
hotty-blitz; not chased yet.

## Tests

- `TestHottyMermaidIsAnSVG`: a flowchart fence becomes a surface whose
  `<img>` has the SVG's size and an `image/svg+xml` resource; a kind the
  renderer refuses stays in cells. The renderer is a stub there.
- `TestSVGSize`; `internal/mermaidsvg`'s own tests run merman for real.

## Demo recipe

In hottyterm, `margin testdata/demo/demo.md`, then `j` down to "The loop":

1. The first start shows the diagrams in cells for about 2.5 s, then as
   SVG. A second start is quicker (wazero's cache).
2. The flowchart, the sequence diagram and the state diagram are drawn in
   margin's colours, no wider than the prose.

Questions: do the diagrams read at that size? Are the edge labels' dark
backgrounds acceptable on hottyterm's background? Is 32 rows the right cap
for a tall one?
