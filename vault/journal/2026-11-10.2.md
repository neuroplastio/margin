## What changed

- `surfhost.go` went from 192 lines to a constructor: `newSurfHost(name, m,
  keys)` makes a `surftea.Host` with `Program: keys`, `NoQuit`, and
  `Surface.NoMenu` set (the nil menu in `WriteFrame` was unsafe: hotty-surf's
  answer to the handoff's item 2). The type `surfHost` is gone; the footer,
  search, palette and `hotSurf` hold a `*surftea.Host`.
- hotty.go: `Surface(h.s, r, clip)` in the Layout's list, `AfterLayout` then
  `Sync` after it (were `sendFirst` and `sync`), `Event(h.s, ev)` for an
  event, `Mine(surface)` to find the Host an event names (it also matches a
  list's or menu's surface, which no pane has).
- The stylesheet: surftea sends it once a Session, so `hotState.surfCSS` and
  `surfSheet` are gone. A `RelayoutMsg` that says the host lost documents now
  calls `surftea.Relayout(h.s, msg)`, so the sheet goes again, ahead of the
  documents.
- Cells: `Lines`, `CellsKey`, `CellsPaste` and `Caret()` in place of the
  adapter's own; the prompt's cursor reads `Caret()`.
- The seam test follows: `surftea.PostMsg`, `Posted`, `Attach`.
- go.mod: surftea is a direct require now. surfmd stays indirect until leg 9.
  Still no `go mod tidy`.

## Not done here

- `CellsClick` is not used: the threads view and the tree pane are still
  hotty-a2ui panes. It comes with legs 7 and 8.
- No Host has a Z, a Density or a Theme of its own; the model's own and the
  host's stay.
