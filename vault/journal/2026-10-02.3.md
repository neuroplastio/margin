## What changed

- **One measuring surface per block.** A measure is now `margin-measure-<n>`:
  its document, its numbered `r=auto` placement, and a `del` straight
  after it. Before this, every block of a wave went through the one
  surface `margin-measure`. A host that answers the placement as it
  arrives (hottyterm) gave the right rows either way. A relay that sizes
  the surface on its next frame (plx) could have measured the next block's
  document under the same name.
- **`MARGIN_HOTTY_LOG=<file>`** traces what margin and the host said:
  `ready` (the host's caps), each measuring wave, every `ack`/`error`, and
  `deaf` when a wave goes unanswered. This found the plx problem below in
  one run.
- **`testdata/demo/`** is a tour of margin: prose with inline markup, a PNG,
  an animated GIF, a flowchart, a sequence diagram, a state diagram, a
  table, task lists, a quote and a code block. `gen.go` beside it draws the
  two images in margin's palette (`cd testdata/demo && go run gen.go`).

## Notes

**plx answered no numbered placement.** In a plx pane the trace read
`ready: mode native` (the plxd answers `a=q` itself), `measure: wave 1, 17
blocks`, and 3 s later `deaf`. plx relayed margin's surfaces, but the host's
replies went to plx, and the plxd never answered a pane program's numbered
placement. Fixed in plexos as HOTTY-PLACE-REPLY-01 (`770e69c`, its D93's
2026-10-02 paragraph), approved by the maintainer and landed with the plexos
session's agreement. With that plx, all 17 measures are answered in about
0.6 s and the page is HTML. Testing it also exposed a startup race: margin
started as a session's *first* program asks before the plx viewer has told
the plxd about the terminal, and gets cells. Started into an existing
session, it doesn't.

Mermaid fences and the GIF's animation are not done yet. Mermaid still
draws the ASCII diagram in cells; a WASM mermaid-to-SVG renderer is in
progress. hottyterm shows a GIF's first frame only; that is hotty-blitz's
to fix, and is in progress.
