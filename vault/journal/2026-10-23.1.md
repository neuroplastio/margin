# 2026-10-23.1 — The gutter bar follows the pointer over prose

Kind: felt (on a hotty host the grey gutter bar marks the paragraph under
the pointer, where it stayed put over prose).
Task: the maintainer asked whether hover in a plx pane lags ("Can you
benchmark hover effects with hotty enabled inside plx?"). The bench found
that over prose the bar did not move at all.
Commit: (this one)

## What changed

- **A block surface takes the pointer** (SPEC §9.3), so motion over prose
  never reached margin's cells and the bar only moved over code, tables and
  the gutter. Every block surface now asks for hover (`v=1`, SPEC §9.4),
  and its `hover` marks the block as cell motion does.
- **An out clears the bar only if its surface set it.** Hovers arrive
  late, at most one a frame, and an out may come after the pointer has
  already moved on. `hotState.hoverFrom` is the surface whose hover set the
  bar. Motion over cells resets it, and an out from any other surface
  leaves the bar alone.

## Tests

`make check` and `make test-race`. `TestHottyHoverMarksTheBlock`: a hover
on a paragraph marks it, and an out clears it. `TestHottyHoverOutComesLate`:
an out from the quote after motion onto a code row keeps the code row, and
an out from the first paragraph after a hover on the quote keeps the quote.
Each test fails with its half of the change taken out.

In a private headless sway (hottyterm at 2400×1500, scale 2) on
`testdata/mermaid.md`, I looked at the shots with the pointer on three
paragraphs and then outside the window, straight in hottyterm and as a plx
pane (plx 97e5087). The bar sits beside the paragraph under the pointer and
is gone when the pointer leaves.

Latency from the pointer's move to the bar's frame, 40 trials each, 0
misses (p50/p90 ms):

| | jump | glide (250 Hz) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hottyterm | 48.7 / 48.9 | 44.5 / 61.0 |
| plx pane | 48.6 / 50.8 | 63.0 / 96.5 |

Over the gutter's cells the bar moves in about 33 ms. A hover costs about one
frame more, since the host sends at most one a frame.

## Notes

- plx caps a pane's frames at 30 fps while nothing types. The plx agent
  has a fix (c0871f93: host events count as input) that is not on main
  yet. The glide rows should be measured again once it lands.
- Blitz lights no `tr:hover` (HOVER is set on the cell only). The hotty
  agent has a mods/blitz patch coming. margin itself has no hover CSS.
