## What changed

- `filetree.surf` (state `Filetree`, in tree.go): a `<list scroll keys="none">`
  of `item`s with `depth`, `branch`, `open`, `selected` (the focused file) and
  `current` (the open document: bold in the accent's tone, no more `▸`); a
  file with marks carries its reviewed/total at the row's end (`small muted
  keep`). `keys="none"`: margin reads every key.
- Folding is margin's (`treeClosed`, by folder path and a slash): `treeHost`
  flattens `m.tree` and leaves out what is under a closed folder. A click on a
  folder, or on its chevron (`@toggle`), folds it; a click on a file selects it
  and gives the pane the keyboard, as before. Folders start open. There are no
  fold keys: the old pane had none, only the mouse (h and left still return to
  the document).
- The pane's keyboard: the list is bright while the pane has margin's focus
  (the surface takes the keyboard with `Focus`), quiet while the document has
  it. A surface cannot be told to give the keyboard back, so a pane that loses
  margin's focus is a new surface (`dropTree`, `treeKeys`); its scroll
  position goes with it and the selection scrolls it back into view.
- Gone: `treePane`, `treeNodes`, `treeLevel`, the `hotty-a2ui` catalog import
  in tree.go, and the pane's click plumbing (`treeClicked` takes a path).
- Tests: the cells tests follow the list (the selection's bar, the current
  file bold, `▾ docs`, progress at the row's end); `TestTreePaneFoldsAFolder`
  clicks a folder's name and its chevron (one fold each, not two).
