## What changed

- **hotty-go 0f4c521** (its main, HOTTY 0.4) for `hotty`, `hottytea` and
  `hottytest`, from 699cfd4 (0.1). The wire is msgpack now (0.2), deltas
  are one op list and `p`/`f`/`v` belong to the surface (0.3); hottytea
  does all of that, so margin's side is the SDK's renames (hotty's
  CHANGES.md, 0.2, "In each SDK"):
  - `r.Err()` is `hotty.Err(r)`; an event's `FitRows()` and `Link()` are
    its fields.
  - `Caps.Sends` is gone: a 0.3 host sends every event, so every block is
    placed with fit, and the `fit` flag on hotState is gone with it.
- Tests: `c.Get(k)` is `hotty.Get(c.Control, k)`; a placement's `Fit` is a
  `hotty.Flag`, `hotty.On`.
