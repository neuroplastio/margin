## What changed

The maintainer exported a review of README.md and pasted the result. Two defects
showed up immediately, both about the same thing: an agent could not reliably
find the block being talked about.

**A list was flattened onto one line and cut mid-sentence.** `blockText` joined
a raw block's lines with spaces, so

    - **Open it in an editor.** … - **Open a pull request.** Comments work, but the review…

was neither readable nor greppable. Raw blocks are now quoted line by line with
their structure intact, capped at 6 lines with an explicit `…`, and paragraphs
truncate on a word boundary rather than mid-word.

**There was no way to locate a block.** The item header was `## ^0afa31`, which
an agent cannot search for, because ids are not stamped into the source yet
(ID-01). Blocks now carry a 1-based line number from the parser, and the header
is `## README.md:12`. Paragraphs also name their enclosing section, and headings
are quoted as markdown (`> ## Budgets`) so the level is visible and the line is
greppable.

Also: exactly one trailing newline. This lands in a paste buffer, and a tail of
blank lines reads as though something went missing.

## Notes

The general principle worth keeping: **until ID-01 lands, quote fidelity is the
whole game.** The anchor is meaningless outside margin's own session, so the
locator is file:line, the section, and a faithful quote. Once ids are stamped
this gets easier, but the quote should stay good — it is what makes the export
readable by a human as well as actionable by an agent.

Nothing here is felt: the defects were reported, not discovered, and the fixes
are all verifiable. The board's `Awaiting review:` stays on PARSE-02.
