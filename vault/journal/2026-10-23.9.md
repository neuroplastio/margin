## What the spec changed

With no terminal attached, a multiplexer keeps a=doc, a=delta, a=field,
a=focus and a=blur, and the rows of an r=auto placement, unchecked
(§3.6). It answers ENOTERM only where the program asks for every answer
(q=0, which n implies). An r=auto placement answered ENOTERM moves the
cursor one row, unless C=1.

## margin against it

- margin numbers only its measure placements and `margin-attach`. Both
  still hear ENOTERM, as D17 needs.
- Every placement goes through PlaceAt, which sets C=1, so the cursor rule
  never applies.
- margin numbers no doc, delta, field, focus or blur, and nor does the kit.
- hottytea does number one: the first document it sends again after a
  loss. With nobody attached, that one is answered ENOTERM. hottytea passes
  that answer on as an ErrorMsg, and margin showed it as a status: "hotty:
  hotty: doc m…: ENOTERM".

## What changed

- go.mod: hotty-go, hottytea and hottytest at v0.5.3.
- An ENOTERM that is not a measure is taken as kept, unchecked, with no
  status. D17 says so, in place.
- The status no longer adds its own "hotty: ": `hotty.Err` already does.
