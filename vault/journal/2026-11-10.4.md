## What changed

- `paletteHostHeight` no longer copies surf.css's pixels (`rule, item = 9,
  8`). `surfRows` (surfhost.go) asks the Surface: `Size(cols, Metrics{cell})`
  then `Rows()`, which is `surfui.Rows`' estimate from the host's cell until
  the host has measured the document, then the host's. A surface not shown yet
  has no tree to count, so `surfRows` shows it once first (the Layout shows it
  again and sends that frame). The composer's host rows use the same.
- The palette is placed with `Fit` (as the composer is): the host says when
  the rows its document needs differ from the ones placed, the event goes to
  the Host, and the frame is placed again at them. So the palette follows its
  matches (seven to one) without margin counting.
- With a 20 px cell the estimate is 12 rows for the rule, seven commands and
  the field: what the hand count gave (`TestPaletteRowsAreEstimated`).

## Not done

- `surfrun.Hold` ("hold the top still" while a surface grows) is not used: the
  palette and the composer sit at the bottom or in the column and margin
  re-lays the document around them. Whether the document jumps is on the look
  list.
