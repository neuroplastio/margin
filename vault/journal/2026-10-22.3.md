# 2026-10-22.3 — A narrow pane keeps the document on screen

Kind: felt (in a terminal or plx pane narrower than 43 columns the prose
wraps at the screen's edge, where it was cut there).
Task: the maintainer's 2026-10-21 report, its third margin-side item: "Go
ahead on the issues you mentioned".
Commit: (this one)

## What changed

- **The measure stops at the screen's edge.** `contentWidth` had a 40-column
  floor and the document column starts past the 3-column gutter, so below
  43 columns every line, and on a HOTTY host every surface (its column is
  `contentWidth` wide), ran past the right edge and was cut. Below the
  floor the measure now gives up the right-hand gutter first, then the floor:
  it is never wider than the screen past the gutter (and the tree pane).
- **A collapsed thread's line keeps to the measure.** Its summary was cut at
  a fixed 52 characters, so it ran to about 70 columns wide whatever the
  screen; in a 60-column pane its end, the reply count, was cut. It is now
  cut with "…" at the measure.

## Tests

`make check` and `make test-race`. `TestContentWidthAdaptsToTheTerminal`
now pins the floor at 44 columns and the screen's edge at 30.
`TestNarrowTerminalKeepsToTheScreen`: at 20, 30 and 42 columns no line of
the seeded review is wider than the terminal. It fails with the old floor
(prose 38 wide at 20 columns), and failed on the thread line before it was
cut. Looked at the cells view at 30 columns: prose wraps at 27, the thread
line ends "glob…", the footer is whole.

## Notes

- A heading does not wrap, so at about 12 columns a heading longer than the
  screen is still cut. That was so before; it is not part of this leg.
- On a host nothing new was looked at: the surfaces' column is
  `contentWidth` wide, so it follows the cells measure.
