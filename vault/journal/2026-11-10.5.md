## What changed

- `threads.surf` (state `Threads`, in inbox.go): a `<table scroll autofocus>`
  of the threads: a state column (`✓`), the document (a tree review only,
  `:if="tree"`, auto, at most 40%), and the thread, which fills, with a
  `block gone` / `doc not in tree` chip where that is so. A resolved row is
  muted. `<empty>` says there are no threads yet. j/k/the arrows/g/G are
  `<bind>`s on the table; a click selects, a click on the selected row opens it
  (`@activate`).
- The selection is still margin's (`inboxAt`): the binds call back to it, and
  Enter, l and right open the focused row from margin (on a host Enter reaches
  margin as a key the table does not act on). In cells margin's keys go to the
  table (`CellsKey`), on a host to `Host.Key`.
- Gone: `inboxPane`, `inboxClicked`, the `hotty-a2ui` catalog import in
  inbox.go, the pane's wheel (`CellsWheel` replaces it).
- The palette's id column is not moved here: it is `<text :width="idw">` in
  `palette.surf` and works; the table would turn the palette's list into a
  table, a different look. Left for a look at the threads table first.
- hotty-surf v0.5.1 (caret on a click in a field, `CellsWheel`): bumped.
  `TestClickInsidePaneDoesNotBlur` pins the caret again, and the wheel over a
  composer in cells scrolls its field first (`TestComposerWheelScrollsItsField`);
  over the threads view it scrolls the table.
- Tests: the cells tests of the view follow the table (the selected row is the
  bar on the selection's ground; "block gone" is a chip), and
  `TestHottyThreadsViewIsASurface` runs it on a host: two comments posted, `i`,
  both threads in the surface, `j`, Enter lands on a block. `hotRunWith` gives
  a test a model setup (a store).
