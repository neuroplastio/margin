# 2026-10-21.1 — Mermaid diagrams drawn by Ondine

Kind: felt (every diagram on a HOTTY host now looks different: monospace
labels, Ondine's layouts, new kinds; the renderer swap itself is mechanical
and tested).
Task: maintainer direction — evaluate ondine.sh for margin, then "Do the
swap" (D24, amending D19).
Commit: (this one)

## What changed

- **`internal/mermaidsvg` runs Ondine** (github.com/wevm/ondine at
  `6a1586e`, MIT) instead of merman: Ondine's own `wasm` crate, every kind,
  its size profile, embedded gzipped at 0.7 MB (merman's was 2.5 MB). The
  module imports nothing; margin writes the source and options into it
  through `alloc`, calls `render`, and decodes one length-prefixed result.
  No WASI, no stdin/stdout.
- **33 kinds** where merman drew 11: quadrant, xychart, sankey, block,
  architecture, C4, kanban, packet, radar, requirement, treemap and the
  rest now come out as SVG on a host instead of staying cells.
- **`flatten.go`** writes margin's palette into the SVG: Ondine themes with
  CSS custom properties and `color-mix()`, which usvg cannot read (F34);
  unflattened, a diagram painted as black boxes with no text.
- **Labels are monospace** at 16px, in Ondine's monospace stack: Ondine
  measures every glyph at 0.6em, so Inter would overrun the boxes.
- **`make mermaid-wasm`** fetches Ondine at `ONDINE_REV` into
  `internal/mermaidsvg/wasm/ondine/` (ignored) and builds it with Ondine's
  own toolchain file and lock. The merman wrapper crate, its Inter metrics
  and `make mermaid-metrics` are gone; `wasm/README.md` describes the
  interface, and `wasm/LICENSE` is Ondine's.
- Records: D24 (amends D19), F34, the board.

## Measured

On `testdata/mermaid.md`, a fresh instance and a render together:

| | merman | Ondine |
|---|---|---|
| a diagram | 25–200 ms | 1–2 ms |
| cold compile (wazero, no cache) | ~2.5 s | ~0.9 s |
| 400-node flowchart | — | ~95 ms |

The first diagram on a cold cache now waits about a second, not two and a
half.

## Tests

`make check` and `make test-race`. The kinds test now includes quadrant,
xychart and sankey, and fails if `var(`, `color-mix(` or a custom property
survives into the SVG or no palette colour does. The on-canvas test follows
`translate()`s, as Ondine places labels in translated groups with a
`<tspan>` per wrapped line. Unknown kinds (`zenuml`) and parse errors are
errors; a parser message is put on one line, caret and blank lines dropped.
`flatten_test.go` covers resolution through fallbacks, `color-mix()` with
one, two or no percentages, nesting, and a cycle.

Painted outside margin with usvg 0.48.1 (hotty-blitz's) on the demo's three
diagrams and `testdata/mermaid.md`'s six: colours, text and sizes come out as
intended there. Not checked in hottyterm or a plx pane: no host here.

## Notes

- Ondine refuses a source past 50,000 characters (mermaid's maxTextSize);
  margin's own 1 MB cap stays, as the bound on what is copied in.
- `responsive`, `links` and `images` are off: margin's stylesheet sizes the
  `<img>`, which follows no link and fetches nothing. `autoDirection` does
  nothing without a `maxWidth`, which margin does not pass.
- The module's `reset` export is unused: each render is a fresh instance.
