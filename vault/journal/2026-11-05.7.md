## What changed

- The `:` palette is a hotty-surf component: `Palette` (palette.go) and
  `palette.surf`: a rule, the commands in view as list items (id, what it
  does, its key as a cap), and ":" with a bare field. A dense surface on a
  host; `cells.Renderer` lines elsewhere, with the terminal's cursor.
- The selection, enter, the arrows, ctrl+j/ctrl+k, tab and esc are margin's
  (`Surface.Program`); typing on a host is the host's field
  (`Palette.Typed` → `paletteQueried`), in cells the renderer's.
- margin keeps the window: `paletteTop` follows the selection, seven rows in
  view, so surf draws only what is shown.
- A click on a row selects it (it did not before either).
- Staging a command (`mark ` and its values) makes a fresh surface, so the
  field takes the seed (`dropPalette`).
- hotty-a2ui no longer draws the palette; the tree pane, the threads view
  and the open thread are what it draws now.

Sheets of the bottom 420 px: `:`, then `:mark`, then `:mark` and two
downs, before and after, at 897 and 1796 on a host and in cells. I looked at
each:

  view and two cells.

  plain `j`, `ctrl+f`, `space`.
- **Mono and dense**, where the a2ui table was proportional and roomy: seven
  commands fit in the rows five did, and none is cut at the bottom.
- The selected row has a bar and a fill; in cells a grey fill.
- The query row sits under the list: ":" accent, the field bare.
- The shot's own typing dropped the first keys of the third state's query on
  the host (`ark`); the cells show `mark`. It is the shot, not margin.
- Not seen: a pane under 897 px, a command id longer than the room.
