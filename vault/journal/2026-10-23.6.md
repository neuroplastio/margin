# 2026-10-23.6 — Waiting for a terminal (ENOTERM)

Kind: mech
Task: the hotty agent's note that the maintainer settled the EBUDGET
question in hotty 9f5950c (SPEC §3.6, §5.2, §13), raised by margin
(journal 2026-10-23.4) and plx.
Commit: (this one)

## What changed

The SPEC now has `ENOTERM`. A multiplexer running a pane with no terminal
attached makes an `r=auto` placement and answers `ENOTERM` straight away,
where the rows would be. With `f=1`, the placement hears its rows as `fit`
once a terminal attaches and lays the surface out. `EBUDGET` goes back to
meaning a render over budget. Until now margin took an unknown error as a
refusal, so once plx sends `ENOTERM`, margin started with no viewer would
have shown cells for good, and input would not have helped.

- **ENOTERM is "not yet", and no try.** The block waits in `later`, as
  EBUDGET's do, but without counting against `hotTries`.
- **One surface waits for the terminal.** margin's measure surfaces are
  deleted in the same batch they are placed in, so they can never hear a
  fit. The first block answered ENOTERM is placed again on
  `margin-attach`, the way a measure is (one cell, z=-1000), with `f=1`,
  and kept. That placement's own ENOTERM is taken quietly, not shown in
  the status line. Its first `fit` gives that block its rows, deletes the
  surface, and asks again for everything not yet measured. A reply with
  rows (a terminal attached before it was answered) does the same.
- After a lost document the next frame places it again. On quit margin
  deletes it, since it was sent outside Layout and Close does not know it.
- hotty-go names no `ENOTERM` constant yet, so margin compares against
  the string (`hotENOTERM`) until the SDK's next patch.
- EBUDGET keeps its three asks on input: plx 1e94151, the one released,
  still answers EBUDGET for a pane it isn't showing.

## Tests

`make check` and `make test-race` pass. `TestHotMeasureWaitsForATerminal`
answers two blocks with ENOTERM. It checks that neither is in cells or
counted as a try, that the frame places the attach surface once, that its
own ENOTERM leaves the status line empty, and that the attach surface's fit
gives the first block its rows and asks for the second again. It fails
without the ENOTERM case, and fails without the fit on `margin-attach`.

Not checked end to end: no host sends ENOTERM yet. plx is getting the SPEC
change, and hottytest's host will follow the SDK.

## Notes

- The ENOTERM retry on input still applies, so a key in a pane with no
  terminal re-asks the wave once and gets ENOTERM again. That is
  harmless, and rare, since such a pane only gets keys an agent sends it.
