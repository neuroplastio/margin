# 2026-11-05.3 — the footer on hotty-surf (leg 2)

Kind: felt (the footer's look on a host and in cells).
Task: porting margin to hotty-surf, leg 2 (vault/knowledge/hotty-surf-port.md).
Commit: (this one)

## What changed

- The footer is a hotty-surf component: `Footer` (footer.go) and its
  template `footer.surf`, generated into `surf_gen.go` (`go generate`).
  On a host it is a dense surf surface placed with the Session
  (`hotState.placeSurf`); elsewhere `cells.Renderer` draws the same tree, and
  `footerHost.line` paints it in the terminal's colours.
- `footerState` returns the mode and its rest as two strings, not Markdown:
  the mode is semibold, the rest muted. The status is plain text now, where
  a pane's Text was Markdown.
- hotty-a2ui no longer draws the footer; the other panes still use it.
- `surfhost.go` gets `sync` (a state change between frames goes out as ops)
  and a copy of surfrun's `cellsSGR` (gap G6).
- go.mod: `surfgen`'s dependencies (cel-lite, x/tools, x/mod) for
  `go generate`.
- Tests: the footer's surface is named `margin-footer` and its root is
  `u-dense`; its keys read `Ctrl+R` as caps; the inbox's "threads" and its
  count are two runs of text.

## Shots (vault/shots/hotty-surf-footer/)

Before and after, from the demo document, at 1796x1153 and 897x1153 logical,
scale 1.6, on hottyterm (host) and with `--no-hotty` (cells), in a run of its
own (isolated, its own sway). Normal, visual (`shift+v`) and the composer
(`c`). I looked at each, and what moved is this:

- **Keys are caps** (surf's `key`), not a plain letter: `⇧+Y`, `Space`, `^+S`
  on the host, `[Shift+Y]` in cells. Before, a lower-case key and a dim dot
  between hints.
- **The mode is semibold**; its rest (`1 block(s)`) muted. Before, both
  were one run of the same weight.
- **The line starts ~4 px left of where it did** at 897 and about where it
  did at 1796 (`pad="1 0"` on the row); the progress is still flush right.
- Cells keep the mode bold and the keys in brackets, the hints a gap of 3 apart
  where a dot was.
- Not looked at: a pane narrower than 897 (see G7), a host other than hottyterm.

## Chosen and rejected

- Chosen: `row grow` of hints, flush left, in order. Rejected: `fit`, which
  runs right to left and flush right (the palette's footer shape): the hints
  came out reversed.
- Chosen: one component for a host and cells. Rejected: a cells line of
  margin's own beside the template.

## Demo recipe

`margin testdata/demo/demo.md` in hottyterm, then `margin --no-hotty` in any
terminal. Press `shift+v`, `c` (then `esc`), `ctrl+r` after touching the file.
Questions: do key caps in the footer read better than plain keys? Does a
semibold mode against a muted rest read at a glance? Does the line sit
where the document's left edge does? Does it cut sensibly in a 40-column pane
(untested here, G7)? Also, from gov: the caps are uppercase (J C E Q) for
lowercase keys, where the document says "press j and k"; and "0/16 reviewed"
moved from proportional to mono.
