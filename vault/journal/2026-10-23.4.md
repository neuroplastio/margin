# 2026-10-23.4 — Measured again once someone looks

Kind: felt
Task: the maintainer's report that hotty disappears from margin, "feels
like it's reproduced whenever margin gets opened on a hidden plx pane".
Commit: (this one)

## What changed

- **An EBUDGET answer to a measure is "not now".** margin took any error
  as the host refusing the block, set its rows to 0, and showed it in cells
  for good at that width. plx answers every measure from a pane it is not
  showing with EBUDGET (F38), so margin started in a background tab showed
  only cells once the tab was shown, until a resize. Such a block is now
  kept for the next input and measured again then, up to three asks
  (`hotTries`), and cells after that. A document that really is over a
  host's budget still ends in cells, just after three tries instead of one.
- **Deaf is no longer for good.** A wave left unanswered for 3s still puts
  everything in cells. Its blocks are now asked again on the next input,
  under the same three-ask limit: plx with no viewer attached answers no
  measure at all.
- **Input** is a key, the mouse (motion too), a paste, or a surface's
  event other than `fit`. A hidden plx pane gets none of these, so nothing
  is sent while no one is looking.

## Tests

`make check` and `make test-race`. `TestHotMeasureAgainOnInput` answers a
measure with EBUDGET and checks that the block is neither in cells nor
asked again on a frame without input, and is asked again on a key. Then it
lets a wave time out, checks deaf, and checks that a pointer move clears
deaf and asks again. Last, a block answered EBUDGET three times ends in
cells and is not asked again. It fails without the EBUDGET case, and fails
without the input trigger.

In plx 1e94151 with hottyterm headless (`bench/hidden2.sh`): margin was
started by `plx pane exec` in a tab made and then left. It got 25 EBUDGETs
while hidden. When the tab was shown, the trace was silent and the shot
showed cells (mermaid as ASCII, images as links). On the first pointer
move the trace has "again: 25 measures", wave 3 got 25 rows, and the shot
after it has the heading, the paragraph and both images as surfaces.

## Notes

- **Shown, margin stays in cells until the user does something.** plx tells
  a pane nothing when it is shown: no replay, no late answer, no `fit`, and
  no focus events. The whole fix is plx's: measure a hidden pane's surfaces
  in the viewer it is attached to, or tell the pane it is shown. Reported
  to the plx agent with this trace.
- Every surface was in cells, so the pointer moving anywhere over margin's
  pane is input. A key is input too.

## Demo

In hottyterm with plx:

1. `plx tab new`, then `plx tab prev` to go back.
2. Start margin in the new tab without showing it:
   `plx pane exec --replace --target <the new tab's pane> -- margin testdata/mermaid.md`.
3. Wait five seconds, then `plx tab next`.

Questions:
- Before you touch anything, are the blocks in cells? (Expected, until plx
  changes.)
- Move the pointer over the pane or press `j`. Do the prose, the images
  and the diagrams turn into surfaces within about a second?
- Is "cells until the first input" acceptable for now, or is it bad enough
  to need a timer instead (one wave every few seconds while hidden)?

Chosen: ask again on input, which costs nothing while no one is looking.
Rejected: a timer, which keeps sending waves to a hidden pane, and backoff,
which makes the user wait once they look.
