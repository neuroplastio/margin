# 2026-11-05.5 — hover re-places every surface (found, not fixed)

Kind: mech (a finding; no code).
Task: maintainer feedback in the f366600 loop, relayed by gov: "moving cursor
over the "try it" block jitters the text a lot".
Commit: (this one)

## Finding

Present in 4c12de7 (before the footer leg) and in f366600: the footer leg did
not bring it in. On a host, some hovers make margin re-place every surface.

Reproduction, a test on a hottytest host over a document shaped like the
demo's "Try it" (a heading, a paragraph, a list with a nested item, a quote):
hover each block's surface in turn and count the commands after each hover.
Five of nine hovers send 10 `a=place` (9 blocks and the footer) and ~590
bytes of cells; the other four send nothing and ~10 bytes. Positions do not
change.

## Cause

The bytes after a re-placing hover: `\r ESC M` (cursor up), `CSI J` (erase to
the end of the screen), the gutter's `▌` on the hovered block's row, then
the place commands. The cells under every block surface and the footer are
blank, so when the hovered block's bar moves Bubble Tea clears to the end
of the screen instead of writing blank lines. The erase covers the surfaces'
cells, so the host drops their placements; hottytea's watcher sees the
erase and the Session clears `placed`; the next `Layout` places everything
again, and the host lays every block surface out again. That is the jitter.
A hover whose erase is one line (`CSI K`), or below the last surface, costs
nothing.

## Fix: held

Any hottytea program that places surfaces over blank cells has this, so gov
asked hotty Agent whether hottytea should own the fix. A candidate in
margin, if it is not: one non-blank cell outside every surface (the last
row's column 0, which the footer's rect leaves free), so the renderer writes
lines instead of clearing to the end. A regression test is the reproduction
above, asserting no `place` after a hover. Board: Backlog — hover.
