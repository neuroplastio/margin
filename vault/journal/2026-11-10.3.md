## What changed

- `compose.surf` (state `Compose`, in composer.go): a `field` of
  `rows="3..8"` that wraps, grows with its text and has the keyboard
  (`autofocus`, `caret="end"`); three buttons under it, Submit (or Save, for
  an edit), Keep draft and Discard; a `stack trap` so Tab walks from the field
  to the buttons and round again in cells. The surface's program keys are
  ctrl+s, ctrl+enter and ctrl+g (`composerFieldKeys`): the field gives them
  back, so they never type. escape is always given back.
- `composer` holds a `*surftea.Host` and the `Compose` state in place of a
  kit pane. A button asks for an outcome (`asked`); `composerActs` applies it
  after the key or event, so a dismiss never happens in the middle of the
  surface's own call.
- Height: in cells, the field's rows as the wrap tells `@height` (three to
  eight) and the bar under it (`composerCellRows`); on a host, the surface is
  placed with `Fit` and its rows are `Surface.Rows()`: hotty-surf's estimate
  from the host's cell until the host's measure (a fit event) comes, then
  that. A fit event for a surface of margin's goes to its Host and the frame
  is placed again at the new rows.
- Gone: `composerKeys`, the TextField/Column/Row definition, `button`,
  `actionOutcome`, the pane's manual re-focus of the field after a host gave
  the keyboard back, and the cells' click and wheel through the pane.
- Tests: `composer_test.go` (paste survives every outcome; a resumed draft
  types at the end; the field grows three to eight rows; Tab walks the
  buttons; a host's fit re-places the composer; words survive escape, ctrl+g
  and ctrl+s on a host). The surf ids in hotty_test.go are surf's
  (`r.kcomposer.kfield`).

## Lost on the way (hotty-surf v0.5.0 limits, told to hotty)

- A click in a field in cells does not place its caret. The old pane did.
  `TestClickInsidePaneDoesNotBlur` now pins that the click keeps the editor
  and its words, not where the caret goes.
- `surftea.Host` has no `CellsWheel`: over the composer in cells the wheel now
  scrolls the document, where it used to scroll the field.
- Nothing worked around in margin.

## Fragile / seen

- `TestHottyHoverWithASelectionRepairsInsteadOfRelaying` is a timing test: on
  a busy machine under `-race` it failed once in three runs with this change
  and once in two on the commit before it; alone it passes (5 of 5 here).
  Run it alone before blaming a change.
- ctrl+enter is not in the host test: hottytest's `Key("ctrl+enter")` did not
  reach margin as the key; the cells test covers it.
