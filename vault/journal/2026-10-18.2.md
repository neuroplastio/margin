## What changed

- **The composer** is a hotty-a2ui `longText` TextField in a pane of its own,
  inside the thread's box, with Submit / Keep draft / Discard buttons under
  it. `ctrl+s` / `ctrl+enter` submit, `esc` or a click away keeps a draft,
  `ctrl+g` discards, `enter` and `shift+enter` are line breaks, `tab` walks to
  the buttons and round again. Dismissal is synchronous; the outcomes and draft
  rules are D3's. On a HOTTY host the composer is an HTML textarea surface with
  the host's keyboard.
- **The panes** (`internal/review/kit.go`): margin is the A2UI agent of each.
  - palette: a HottyTable (`command · does · key`, seven rows) over the
    query's field;
  - search prompt: the draft's field and `n/m`;
  - footer: the mode, a HottyKeyHints help line from labelled
    HottyShortcuts, the progress, the status;
  - tree pane: a HottyTree, `▸` on the open file, `n/m` (`!` if flagged)
    after the name;
  - threads view: a HottyTable, the selected row reversed, a click selecting;
  - an open thread's comments: a head over Markdown text, margin keeping
    the focus bar beside them.
  On a host every pane with a rectangle of its own is an HTML surface
  (`hotState.place`); the thread's comments, content-tall, stay cells.
- **Gone**: nvim, the pty, x/vt, creack/pty, the key encoders,
  `scripts/setup-env.sh`, `make doctor`, CI's nvim install, `$EDITOR` and
  `MARGIN_NVIM_CONFIG`. The board's "(composer shift+enter)" look went with
  them: there is no nvim to misread it.
- Records: D22 (supersedes D2, D3; amends D17); interaction.md's composer and
  dismissal sections keep what carried over and list the new specifics as not
  settled; findings F29–F33; goals.md's principle 2; README; AGENTS.md and
  the do-leg/do-run skills.

## Tests

`make check` and `go test -race ./...`, no nvim installed. The composer tests
drive the model synchronously now; host tests (`hottytest`) cover the composer
(fill, `ctrl+s`, `esc`, the Discard button), the palette and the search
prompt as surfaces with the keyboard. A smoke run of the binary in a pty typed
and posted a comment and filtered the palette.

## Kit gaps met

- A longText field does not soft-wrap in cells; a long line scrolls sideways
  (profile §3.5). On a host the textarea wraps.
- Text sets paragraphs flush and folds single newlines (F29); a backslash
  escape is drawn as written, so a status can't be kept from being Markdown.
- HottyTable always draws a header row and its rule.
- Pre-existing, not the kit's: the palette's fuzzy match takes no literal dot
  (`view.raw` matches nothing; `raw` does).

## Demo

```
make build
./bin/margin --no-hotty testdata/sample.md     # cells
./bin/margin testdata/sample.md                # in hottyterm: HTML panes
```

1. `j` to a paragraph, `c`, type two lines (enter between), `ctrl+s`. Does the
   field read as the place to type? Is a 3–8 row field enough, and is the
   sideways scroll of a long line in cells livable?
2. `c` again, type, `esc`; `e` on the draft brings it back with the cursor at
   the end. `ctrl+g` discards. Are esc-keeps and ctrl+g-discards the right
   keys? Are the three buttons worth their row?
3. `:` — the palette table. Type `mark`, arrows, enter. Does the header row
   earn its two rows? Does the `command · does · key` layout read?
4. `/retry`, enter. The prompt's field and count.
5. The footer: `j move • c comment • …` then progress and status, no colour.
   Is the lost green/orange missed?
6. `margin testdata/` (a directory): the folding tree, `▸`, progress after
   the name; `i` for the threads table, click a row.
7. Open a thread with a multi-paragraph comment: Markdown, a blank row between
   paragraphs, the `▌` beside the focused comment.
8. In hottyterm: the composer, palette, prompt, footer, tree and threads view
   as HTML. Do they sit right in their rows?

Chose: the document column stays margin's (the kit can't carry its marks,
